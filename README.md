# Hi, I'm Nicolás

I'm a bioinformatician and evolutionary biologist from Argentina, now based in Valencia, Spain.

Studying bioinformatics gave me strong technical tools, but I soon felt I was missing the biology behind the data. 
That’s why I did my PhD in evolutionary biology, and why most of what you’ll find here started as a biological question.

During my PhD I assembled the genomes of cactophilic *Drosophila*, flies that breed in rotting cactus tissue and feed on the
yeasts and microorganisms that grow there. Then, as a postdoc, I sequenced three species of cactus moths, whose larvae feed on the commercially important prickly pear and the culturally significant cardón. Field sampling took us across the Quebrada de Humahuaca, a UNESCO World Heritage Site in northwestern Argentina, including the Pucará de Tilcara. 

Since 2024, I've worked in industry: first with a synthetic biology startup developing a protein expression platform in black soldier fly, and now with Cannformatics, a health-tech company in Silicon Valley, building pipelines that test AI-generated hypotheses against clinical and biological evidence.

I have also dedicated a good part of my career to teaching, adapting the same ideas to very different audiences: 
- bioinformatics, Python and genomics for university students;
- health informatics, health information systems, digital medical data, handling of sensitive personal information and AI for future health professionals;
- bioinformatics workshops for students and early-career researchers through [RSG Argentina](https://rsg-argentina.netlify.app/);
- generative AI for colleagues at a startup.

## Selected work

- [BUSCO2Tree](https://github.com/niconm89/BUSCO2Tree): builds phylogenomic trees from BUSCO single-copy genes (MAFFT, trimAl, IQ-TREE2), with Docker and Nextflow support. Example data from [Moreyra et al. 2023, *Molecular Phylogenetics and Evolution*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2022.107653).
- [pipeFSC](https://github.com/niconm89/pipeFSC): automates demographic inference with fastsimcoal2. Used in [Poveda-Martínez et al. 2024, *Biological Invasions*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10530-024-03317-2).
- [NDEV](https://github.com/niconm89/NDEV): nucleotide diversity along alignments in sliding windows. Used in [Moreyra et al. 2019, *PLoS ONE*](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0220676).
- [buzzatii-cluster_genomes](https://github.com/niconm89/buzzatii-cluster_genomes): code behind the genome assemblies and phylogenomics of [Moreyra et al. 2023, *Molecular Phylogenetics and Evolution*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ympev.2022.107653).
- [scripts_bioinformatics](https://github.com/niconm89/scripts_bioinformatics): small utilities for sequence, alignment and annotation files.
